# LA Solarpunk

Welcome!

We're a Los Angeles-based volunteer group working to build the future that solarpunk promises. That means renewable energy, mutual aid, community resilience, and technology that actually serves people. We believe the tools exist to do this work right now, and we're trying to put them to use.

## What We Do
 
Our approach is grounded in partnership. Rather than building things in a vacuum, we work alongside existing community organizations and mutual aid groups to understand what they need and develop real solutions alongside them. The projects we take on span software and hardware, and we tend to skew toward the physical side of things: permaculture, energy systems, embedded devices, and infrastructure that communities can own and maintain themselves.
 
We're also trying to create a space for people in tech who want to do work that matters. If you love building things but have felt disconnected from the broader impact of what you make, this is a place to change that.
 
## Getting Involved
 
You don't need to be a developer or an engineer to be a part of this. There's always room for organizers, writers, designers, and anyone who cares about where this is all headed.
 
That said, if you do write code or work with hardware, we'd love your help. A good first step is to:
 
1. Check out issues in the the start-here repository to get an idea of what we're currently doing
2. Open an issue or leave a comment if something catches your eye
3. Reach out to us on Discord (link below) to see how you can get involved!

We try to keep our issues well-documented and welcoming to newcomers, and we're happy to help orient anyone who's just getting started.

## Where to Find Us
We currently meet at [CRASH Space](https://blog.crashspace.org/) on the first and third Thursday of every month. There's a virtual option as well if you want to join remotely, just check out the CRASH space calendar.

We do most of our discussions in the [CRASH space discord](https://discord.com/channels/1307409684390608946/1410738506275229839). Feel free to join and introduce yourself.

We're an experimental group! We're trying and exploring new ways of doing things and we're doing our best to not let perfect be the enemy of better. If you have ideas for how we can improve processes or projects, let us know!

Let's make something good together!
